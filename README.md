We explore factors other than credit grade that explain delinquency or default risk and examine whether their associations with risk vary over the life of the loan.

RQ1: How do Kaplan–Meier curves based on proxy event times differ by credit grade?

RQ2: Does each literature-based covariate group (A, B, and C) provide additional explanatory information after accounting for credit grade and origination year?

RQ3: (How) do the associations for key factors in each covariate group differ between the early and late periods?
